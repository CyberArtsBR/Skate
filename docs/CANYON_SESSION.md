# Canyon Session

Replaces rejected Storm Coast with a traditional fixed image background. No parallax, water, rain, lightning or storm ambience. Existing ramp, controls, physics and character presentation are preserved. The same generated image is used in selection and gameplay through MAP_IMAGES, with content hash versioning to invalidate cached replacements. Stored Storm Coast selections migrate to Canyon Session.

Theme: sunny dry red-rock canyon, desert vegetation, distant festival town, level overlook with low fence. Authored warm daylight profile and dry apron colors match the background. Uses the existing daylight reflection environment and quiet outdoor ambience.

Source: built-in image_gen creation. Native asset resolution 1672x941 PNG (2,520,849 bytes). The generator did not return the requested 3840x2160 size; this is not a 4K asset. No upscaling or format conversion. The image has no baked ramp, character, HUD, water or storm. Reuses existing visual ramp source.

Prompt: detailed stylized 3D dry canyon at golden afternoon; mesas, small distant arid town, Joshua trees/agave, low fence and concrete overlook, broad clear sky; no text, people, ramp, water or storm.

Static review only. No tests, benchmarks or local build. User review needed for background crop and lighting on their screen.
